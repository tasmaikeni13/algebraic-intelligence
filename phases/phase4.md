## 2. Mathematical Formulations & Zero-Transcendental Constraints

### 2.1 Strictly Proper $\alpha$-Algebraic Power Score
For $\alpha\in(0,1)$, target distribution $\mathbf y$, and prediction $\mathbf p\in\operatorname{int}\Delta^{K-1}$, use the Bregman score generated by the convex entropy $-\sum_i p_i^{1-\alpha}/[\alpha(1-\alpha)]$:
$$
\mathcal L_\alpha(\mathbf p,\mathbf y)=
\frac1\alpha\sum_i y_i p_i^{-\alpha}
+\frac1{1-\alpha}\sum_i p_i^{1-\alpha}
-\frac1{\alpha(1-\alpha)}\sum_i y_i^{1-\alpha}.
$$
For $\alpha=1/8$:
$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{OACE}}=
8\sum_i y_i p_i^{-1/8}+\frac87\sum_i p_i^{7/8}-\frac{64}{7}\sum_i y_i^{7/8}.
$$
The correction terms are mandatory. The older local expression $8(p_k^{-1/8}-1)$ is classification-calibrated for a single hard label but is **not** strictly proper in expectation; for soft targets its optimum is $p_i\propto y_i^{8/9}$.

Evaluate $p_i^{-1/8}$ using exactly three sequential `rsqrt` instructions:
$$z_1=\operatorname{rsqrt}(p_i)=p_i^{-1/2},\quad z_2=\operatorname{rsqrt}(z_1)=p_i^{1/4},\quad z_3=\operatorname{rsqrt}(z_2)=p_i^{-1/8},$$
then $p_i^{7/8}=p_i z_3$. This corrects the former sign error in the documented intermediate exponent.

### 2.2 Gradient Domains & Fisher Curvature
- **Probability gradient:** $\partial\mathcal L/\partial p_i=p_i^{-1/8}-y_i p_i^{-9/8}$ (before the optional calibration factor $\gamma$). It is singular as $p_i\to0$ and must never be reported as the smaller score-space expression.
- **Composed score gradient:** AVN bounds A-Softmax inputs and therefore gives every $p_i$ a positive real lower bound. The complete `oace_loss(algebraic_softmax(scores), target)` gradient is finite; this composed path is the training-stability contract.
- **Pearson $\chi^2$ Equivalence:** For target distribution $\mathbf{y}$, the algebraic divergence $D_A(\mathbf{y} \| \mathbf{p}) = \sum \frac{y_i^2}{p_i} - 1$ is non-negative and convex, with Hessian $H(D_A)|_{\mathbf{p}=\mathbf{y}} = 2 H(D_{\text{KL}})|_{\mathbf{p}=\mathbf{y}}$.
